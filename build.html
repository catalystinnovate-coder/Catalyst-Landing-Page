BUILD is Catalyst's organisational design and operations pillar. The internal engine: org design, SOPs, financial controls, hiring frameworks, and governance. The foundation everything else runs on.

What is BUILD

A business can only grow as fast as its infrastructure allows. When the structure breaks, everything stalls, including the founder.

BUILD is the first layer of sustainable business development. It works with founders who are running fast but building on unstable ground. When the infrastructure cannot support the pace, the founder absorbs the load. That is not a leadership problem. It is a structural one.

The work here is precise. Turning a founder-dependent operation into a structured, scalable company. Defining who owns what, how decisions get made, and how the business moves when the founder is not in the room.

What BUILD creates is a company that can scale. Operational systems that hold under pressure. Financial controls that give the founder real visibility. A structure that enables the team to perform at their level without constant intervention.

  • Organisational structure and team design that fits where you're going
  • Hiring frameworks that bring in the right people consistently
  • SOPs that remove the founder from decisions they should not be in
  • Financial controls and management reporting that give real visibility
  • Role clarity and accountability structures that enable the team
  • Scaling frameworks that prepare you for the next phase before it arrives
Our Approach
How BUILD works.
01

Organisational Diagnostic

Map the current state: decision-making patterns, accountability gaps, structural cracks, and founder dependencies. Know exactly what is holding the business back before designing what comes next.

02

Org Structure and Role Design

Design a structure that fits where the business is going, not just where it is. Clear roles, defined ownership, and team architecture that enables performance rather than managing around confusion.

03

Systems and Process Architecture

SOPs, workflows, approval processes, and decision frameworks. Build the operating infrastructure that lets the team move without the founder being the answer to every question.

04

Financial Controls and Management Reporting

Budget frameworks, cash flow models, and forward-looking management instruments. Financial visibility that supports decision-making, not just reporting after the fact.

05

Hiring Frameworks and Onboarding

Job architecture, competency frameworks, and interview structures. A repeatable system for bringing the right people in and integrating them without the founder carrying that load alone.

06

Scale Readiness and Transition Planning

Prepare for the next phase of growth before it arrives. Identify what the business will need at the next level of scale and build toward it while the current structure is still functioning.

Who It's For
Recognise any of these?
Signal One
Everything still goes through you

Every significant decision, every client issue, every operational call lands on your desk. The business cannot move without you. That is not leadership. That is a structure problem.

Signal Two
You're scaling headcount but not output

More people. More payroll. The same, or worse, results. When adding capacity does not add performance, the system is broken. Hiring more people into a broken system makes it worse.

Signal Three
You don't have real financial visibility

You know the revenue. You are less clear on the margins, the cash position in 90 days, or which parts of the business are actually profitable. That is a risk the business should not be carrying.

Signal Four
Your processes exist only in people's heads

When a key person leaves, so does the knowledge. Nothing is written down. Nothing is repeatable. Every time something goes wrong, you are solving it from scratch.

Why Catalyst
Operations consulting built by operators.

Most operations consultants design systems from a distance. They map the current state, hand over a document, and call it done. Catalyst builds from the inside. The co-founders have personally built operational infrastructure from the ground up, in their own organisations and in the organisations they were hired to fix.

The background includes nonprofit COO leadership, corporate banking risk and compliance, and large-scale organisational transitions. That experience changes what gets built. Systems designed by people who have operated them hold differently from systems designed by people who have only advised on them.

BUILD is not a consulting engagement with a handover at the end. The team works with you, implements alongside you, and stays until the structure holds. The measure of success is not a framework delivered. It is a business that runs without you in every room.

  • Operational infrastructure built by people who have built it themselves
  • COO experience leading large-scale nonprofit and organisational transformation
  • Corporate banking risk and compliance background applied to business controls
  • Work with you and alongside you until the structure holds
  • People infrastructure expertise built on real hiring and team-building experience
  • Governance frameworks designed for the way founders actually make decisions
