About Catalyst

Built by founders.
For founders.

Catalyst Innovation Solutions is a Pretoria-based management consultancy working with founder-led businesses at the point of scale — where growth stops being about hustle and starts being about structure.

Origin Story
“We sat in the seat before we built the practice.”

Carmen Naidoo and Maryke Fischer didn’t come from consulting. They built businesses. They hired teams, hit walls, made payroll by the skin of their teeth, and navigated the particular chaos that only founders know. Catalyst was built on that lived experience — not theory.

They founded Catalyst because they kept seeing the same pattern: brilliant founders with real momentum, stuck in the gap between where they were and where they needed to be. Not for lack of ambition — but for lack of structure, systems, and leadership clarity.

Catalyst exists to close that gap. Not with generic frameworks, but with deeply specific, integrated work that meets a business exactly where it is.

How We Operate

The principles that guide every engagement.

01
Structure before scale

We never skip the foundation. Growth without structure is organised chaos — and chaos is expensive. Every engagement starts by identifying what needs to be built before we can grow.

02
No work without contracts

Every engagement starts with clear terms, deliverables, and accountability on both sides. Not because we don’t trust our clients — but because clarity protects the relationship and the result.

03
Results over narrative

We don’t do fluffy strategy decks. Every recommendation comes with an execution plan, defined success metrics, and a clear owner. Good ideas that don’t move are just expensive documents.

04
Integrated by design

BUILD, LEAD, and GROW don’t work in isolation — and neither do we. The most effective engagements address multiple pillars simultaneously, because that’s how businesses actually function.
