Work / Indigo & Oak
03 — 2025
Brand Strategy · Identity Design

Building a Brand From Nothing.

Indigo & Oak · Luxury Personalised Gifting · USA
Context

Indigo & Oak · Luxury Personalised Gifting · USA came to Catalyst for brand strategy · identity design.

The Real Problem

A Vision. No Identity. No Voice.

Indigo & Oak came to us as an idea — a personalised gifting company with a clear vision but no identity, no voice, and no visual language. The challenge was to build everything from the ground up for a competitive gifting market.

Our Approach

We built the complete brand identity from scratch — positioning strategy, voice architecture, visual system, and packaging design direction. The brand was positioned above mass-market gifting but below inaccessible luxury. Every touchpoint was built around gifting as an act of love, not commerce.
